### Hafiyyan Sayyid Fadhlillah

Hafiyyan Sayyid Fadhlillah is a postdoctoral researcher in the [DiverSE](https://www.diverse-team.fr/) team at [University of Rennes](https://www.univ-rennes.fr/en). He earned his bachelor and master degree in Computer Science from [Faculty of Computer Science Universitas Indonesia](https://cs.ui.ac.id/) and a doctorate degree from [Johannes Kepler University Linz](https://www.jku.at/).

His research aims to tame the complexity of developing software-intensive systems through variability management, model-driven engineering and, more recently, providing live modelling features in Domain-Specific (Modelling) Languages.
