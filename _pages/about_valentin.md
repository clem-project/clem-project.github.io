### Valentin Bourcier

Valentin Bourcier is a postdoctoral researcher in the [DiverSE](https://www.diverse-team.fr/) team at [Inria](https://inria.fr/) Rennes. He earned a diploma in software engineering from IMT Atlantique Nantes (formerly École des Mines de Nantes) and a Ph.D. from the University of Lille.

His research aims to improve the experience of developers by facilitating the debugging process and, more recently, by providing live programming features in new programming languages.
