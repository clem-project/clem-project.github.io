### Jean-Baptiste Döderlein

Jean-Baptiste Döderlein is a PhD student in in the [DiverSE](https://www.diverse-team.fr/) team at [University of Rennes](https://www.univ-rennes.fr/en). He obtained his Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees at [ENS Rennes](https://www.ens-rennes.fr/).

His research focuses on language tooling, and more particularly on live programming environments. He works on tools that help developers better understand and explore software systems by making the relationship between code and execution more interactive and accessible.
