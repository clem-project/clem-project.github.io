### MohammadHadi Dehghani

MohammadHadi Dehghani is a doctoral researcher at the Institute of Business Informatics – Software Engineering at [JKU Linz](https://se.jku.at/). In the CLEM project, he is the PhD researcher working on exploratory modeling, design space exploration, and model exploration for behavioral models, supervised by Prof. Manuel Wimmer.

His work develops exploratory modeling as a practical approach to direct manipulation, enabling modelers to compare design alternatives, navigate the design space, and synthesize model solutions during interactive execution. His broader interests span model-driven engineering, software language engineering, process mining, and AI-supported modeling, including large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and AI agents applied to model exploration and search-based software engineering. He was previously a Marie Skłodowska-Curie ITN Fellow in the Lowcomote project.
